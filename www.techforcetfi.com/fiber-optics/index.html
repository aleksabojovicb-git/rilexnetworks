Fusion Splicing Done Right

Fusion splicing joins two fiber optic cables permanently using an electric arc — it's the highest-quality, lowest-loss method of connecting fiber. Rilex Networks technicians are trained and equipped with professional-grade fusion splicing equipment to handle both single mode and multi-mode fiber on any size project.

Every splice is tested with an OTDR (Optical Time Domain Reflectometer) to verify signal integrity. You get the test results as part of your project documentation.
