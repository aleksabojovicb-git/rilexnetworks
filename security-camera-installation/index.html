What We Install

Complete Security Solutions

Access Control Systems

Card readers, keypads, biometric systems, and door controllers that restrict and monitor who enters your facility. From single-door installs to multi-building enterprise systems, Rilex Networks sizes the solution to your operation.

IP and Analog Camera Systems

Indoor and outdoor camera installation covering parking lots, lobbies, warehouses, and commercial facilities. IP and analog systems, NVR and DVR configurations, and remote viewing setup so you can monitor from anywhere.

Intrusion Detection

Motion sensors, door and window contacts, glass break detectors, and alarm panel installation. Integrated with your camera and access control system for a unified security response when it matters most.

Access Control
