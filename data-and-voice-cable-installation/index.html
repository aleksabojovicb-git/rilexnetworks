Full-Range Cabling Services

Structured Cabling

Cat5e, Cat6, and Cat6A copper cabling installations for computer networks, voice systems, and any IP-based infrastructure. Fully tested and certified to EIA/TIA-568 standards on every job.

IT Closet Buildouts

New IT closet construction, existing closet retrofits, and full IT closet relocations. We've resurrected disaster-zone closets and built clean, organized infrastructure rooms from the ground up across the Tri-State area.

WiFi Access Point Deployment

Full wireless infrastructure design and installation. We build your WiFi network to deliver the speeds and coverage your company needs — anywhere in your space, without being tied to a patch cord.

Data Center Builds

From new-construction data centers to complete equipment relocations, Rilex Network manages every phase. Rack installation, cable management, patch panel termination, and full documentation on completion.
